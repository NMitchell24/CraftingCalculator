# Actions and Navigation

This app has two bars for **Actions** and **Navigation**. Where they are and how they look will differ depending on 
the device you are using.

* **Actions:** things you can do with the screen you are on. Adding an item, saving something, and the like. These 
  are different for most pages, so the icons and buttons available will change as you use the app.

* **Navigation:** how you move between the three primary screens.

## The Navigation Bar

The navigation bar exists on every page and gives you the ability to navigate to the three main screens of the 
app. See the table below for an overview of these options.

| What's the option                            | Where does it go                                  |
|----------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| ![Craft](assets/calculate.svg) **Craft**     | Takes you to the [Craft Screen](craft-screen.md)  |
| ![Favorites](assets/star.svg) **Favorites**  | Takes you to the [Favorites Screen](favorites.md) |
| ![Dataset](assets/menu-book.svg) **Dataset** | Takes you to the [Dataset Screen](dataset.md)     |

## The Actions Bar

The actions bar options will change depending on which screen you are looking at. Some pages offer Add and Delete 
options. Other pages may contain shortcuts to access the items that list on the screen. On any page you can tap the 
![Help](assets/help-outline.svg) icon in the top right to see what the actions for that page do.

## When there's more than fits

A phone only has so much room for thumbs. When a screen has more actions than the bar can show, the last slot turns
into ![More](assets/more-vert.svg) **More actions**, and tapping it opens a menu with the rest.

| What device are you using | How many actions you see                                                          |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| A phone, either way up    | Up to 4. With 5 or more you get 3, and ![More](assets/more-vert.svg) has the rest. |
| A tablet                  | As many as fit down the side of the screen, and never fewer than a phone gets.    |
| A PC                      | All of them. If the window is too short, the side menu scrolls on its own.        |

> **Example:** The [Dataset Screen](dataset.md) has 5 actions. On a phone you'll see Categories, Components and
> Blueprints, and ![More](assets/more-vert.svg) holds Import/Export and Delete all data.

## How these bars behave across devices

The **Actions Bar** and **Navigation Bar** will change position depending on the device you are using. The table 
below describes where you will find these bars for the different layouts.

| What device are you using | Where is it                    |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| A phone in portrait mode  | On the bottom of the screen    |
| A phone in landscape mode | On the left side of the screen |
| A tablet or PC            | On the left side of the screen |

## Collapsing the side menu

On larger tablets and desktop windows (at least 960px wide and 600px high), the left side menu expands to show labels
next to its icons. That's handy while you're learning what everything does, and a waste of space once you know.

Tap ![Collapse](assets/menu-open.svg) **Collapse menu** in the top left corner to shrink it down to icons. Tap
![Expand](assets/menu.svg) **Expand menu** to bring the labels back. The app remembers which one you picked, even
after you close it.

Make the window smaller than that and you get the icons-only menu no matter what you picked. Make it big again and
your choice comes back.
