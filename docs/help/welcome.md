# Welcome to Crafting Calculator

You know the moment. You have decided to build the thing. The big thing. A stone fortress in **Valheim**, enough rockets and C4 to foundation wipe that massive clan compound in **Rust**, a beacon in **Minecraft**, The Godbreaker Armor Set in **Conan Exiles**. You open the crafting bench, and the game cheerfully informs you that you need 4 of something that needs 12 of something else that needs a truly upsetting quantity of ore.

So you do what everyone does: you tab out, you find a wiki, you open the calculator app, you lose count, you mine the wrong thing for forty minutes.

This app is the fix. You teach it your game once, and from then on you point at what you want to build, say how many, and it tells you exactly what to go get.

## Three words, and then you know the whole app

Everything here is built out of three kinds of record. That's it. There's no fourth thing.

| Word          | What it means                                                                               | In your game                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |-------------------------------------------|
| **Component** | Raw stuff. Something you gather, mine, kill, farm or buy. It is never broken down further.  | Wood, Stone, Iron Ore, Fiber, Leather     |
| **Blueprint** | A recipe. A list of components — and, if you like, other blueprints — that makes something. | Iron Nails, Fine Wood Bow, Low Grade Fuel |
| **Category**  | A label you stick on components and blueprints so you can find them later.                  | Ores, Food, Base Building, Tier 3 Gear    |

The clever part is that fourth column of the Blueprint row: **a blueprint can contain other blueprints**. Iron Nails need Iron. A Longship needs Iron Nails. You describe each recipe once, exactly the way the game states it, and the app does the rest of the arithmetic on your behalf, all the way down to the ore.
