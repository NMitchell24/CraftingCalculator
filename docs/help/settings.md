# Settings

The ![Settings](assets/settings.svg) icon in the top bar, from anywhere in the app. Tap it again to go
back to whatever you were doing — it remembers where you came from.

It is a short screen. Here is all of it.

## Appearance

Three choices for the app's theme:

- **Follow system** — matches your phone or desktop. Goes dark when your device does, including on a
  schedule if your device is set up that way. This is the default and it is the right answer for almost
  everyone.
- **Light** — always light, whatever the device says.
- **Dark** — always dark. For playing at 2am without lighting up the room.

The change is instant. There is nothing to save and nothing to restart.

## About

The app's name, its version and build number, the copyright, and the licence.

Crafting Calculator is free software under the **GPL 2.0**. You can use it, share it and modify it; the
full terms are summarised on the screen.

The **fonts** note is there because both typefaces ship inside the app rather than being downloaded:
**Inter** for body text and **Jersey 20** for headings, both under the SIL Open Font License. That is
why the app looks the same offline as online — it never fetches anything.

## Where your data lives

Everything you create — every component, blueprint, category and favorite — is stored in a database
**on your device**. That means:

- **No account.** There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into.
- **No internet.** The app makes no network calls at all. It works in a tent, on a plane, in a basement.
- **No telemetry.** Nothing about you or your dataset is collected or sent anywhere.

It also means the honest warning: **your dataset lives and dies with the app's storage.** There is no
cloud copy and no built-in export yet, so uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from your device's
settings, takes your recipes with it. Worth knowing before you spend an evening entering a modpack.

If you want a copy of a batch outside the app today, the ![Copy](assets/content-copy.svg) **Copy**
buttons on the Craft screen's Components and Surplus tabs put the list on your clipboard, ready to
paste anywhere.

## Getting help

The ![Help](assets/help-outline.svg) icon, next to the ![Settings](assets/settings.svg) in the top bar,
opens the help page for whichever screen you are on. From there, the contents list gets you to every
other page — including [How the Math Works](calculations.md), which is the one people usually want.
